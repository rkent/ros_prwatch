Definitions

  • Response: a comment or submitted review by someone other than the PR author. Bot comments are ignored.
  • Committer: anyone who has merged at least one PR in the tracked repos, including in the last 30 days.
  • Waiting PR: an open, non-draft PR where the author (counting the PR opening) made the last comment more than 10 days ago.
  • Recently answered PR: a PR whose author waited more than 10 days, where the wait ended with a response (or a merge or close by someone else) in the last 30 days. For a committer response, the wait runs from the author's last comment to the first committer response after it.
  • PRs authored by bots are excluded. Response-time statistics cover only closed PRs that received a response.
